<!-- section:overview ~22s -->
Nectar is the liquidation liquidity network for lending markets that need reliable execution when collateral turns underwater.

Our product hypothesis is simple and powerful: professional buyers post funded, time-bounded bids for distressed collateral, and those bids are consumed atomically inside the liquidation itself. One transaction repays debt, delivers collateral, and records fees. No hand-waving. No partial promises.

<!-- section:markets ~28s -->
On Markets you see every admitted venue, oracle status, and borrower positions moving from healthy to at-risk to liquidatable. This testnet slice uses mock lending and mock prices, clearly labeled, so operators can rehearse real execution paths without touching production systems.

<!-- section:liquidity ~45s -->
Liquidity is where makers fund segregated cash accounts in the debt token, then publish a quote that reserves the full cash obligation onchain. Until that reservation is included, the bid is not executable capacity. Makers sign exact economics: collateral amount, maximum debt repayment, keeper compensation, and protocol fees.

Watch the flow: mint demo tokens from the faucet, approve and deposit maker cash, then register a funded quote scoped to a borrower position. That quote is now live inventory for keepers.

<!-- section:stress ~22s -->
The testnet stress scenario drops mock oracle prices within bounded limits, simulating a sharp move in tokenized stock collateral. Positions cross the liquidation threshold. Nectar's executor refuses stale or invalid prices; here the route stays actionable because the mock feed is fresh and labeled.

<!-- section:executions ~35s -->
Our open keeper agent monitors the same state as the app, simulates each job, explains refusals, and submits the best eligible quote. The receipt shows debt repaid, collateral delivered to the maker, keeper fee paid, and protocol revenue, reconciled onchain.
